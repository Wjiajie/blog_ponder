I'm **{SITE.author}**, a software engineer with a background in graphics
and visual effects rendering. I'm spending the next six months transitioning into
a **Forward Deployed Engineer** role, with a focus on the **education** vertical.

## Why FDE

The best engineers I know are not the ones with the most abstract knowledge —
they are the ones who can sit in a customer's messy reality and ship something
that actually helps. That is what an FDE does. I want to be that kind of engineer.

## Why education

I've spent years rendering pixels. The shift to rendering *understanding* —
helping a teacher see a student's specific misconception, helping a student
unstuck on a single problem — feels like the right next chapter.

## Why this site looks the way it does

This site borrows from a few people whose work I admire. The article list is
shaped after [Shu Ding](https://shud.in)'s ideas waterfall. The home
page takes cues from [Delba de Oliveira](https://delba.dev)'s clean
two-column layout. The article body and footnote system follow
[Lee Robinson](https://leerob.io)'s typography.
